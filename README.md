# StarX – Digital Growth Strategy
### Organic & Paid Media | CRM Automation | Conversational Commerce

## Project Overview

StarX is a sauce and condiment brand used as the focus of an academic digital marketing project developed by Learning Team 11 at the Fortune Institute of International Business (FIIB).

The project develops an integrated digital growth strategy designed to improve brand awareness, attract business leads, nurture prospective customers, and encourage repeat purchases. It focuses on both B2B audiences, such as restaurants, commercial kitchens, retailers, and wholesalers, and B2C consumers discovering the brand.

The strategy connects digital customer acquisition with CRM automation and conversational commerce to create a more structured customer journey.

## Project Objectives

- Build an organic and paid digital acquisition strategy.
- Improve brand discovery through SEO and social media.
- Reach potential wholesale buyers through paid advertising.
- Design a marketing funnel covering awareness, consideration, and conversion.
- Organise leads using customer segmentation and lifecycle stages.
- Develop automated email journeys to nurture prospects.
- Design chatbot workflows for product enquiries, bulk orders, FAQs, and customer support.
- Connect marketing activities with measurable performance indicators.

## Phase 3: Organic & Paid Media Execution

### 1. Organic Digital Infrastructure

**Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)**

Developed a keyword-led content strategy around high-intent commercial searches, including:
- Bulk sauce supplier
- Wholesale sauces in India
- Sauce supplier for restaurants
- 5 kg sauce packs
- Condiment supplier

The strategy maps search intent to relevant blog content to improve discoverability among restaurants, wholesalers, and potential customers.

**Social Media Strategy**

Proposed a platform-specific approach:
- **Instagram:** Visual brand building, product storytelling, recipe inspiration, and consumer engagement.
- **Facebook:** Community engagement, food-service communication, and B2B audience development.

Content themes include product quality, hygiene, bulk-pack solutions, and the brand's promise of taste and happiness.

### 2. Paid Media Strategy

**Meta Ads – Brand Awareness**

Designed an awareness campaign concept using brand-focused creative assets across feeds and Stories. The objective was to introduce StarX to new audiences and improve brand recall.

**Google Ads – Wholesale Lead Generation**

Developed a high-intent acquisition strategy using Google advertising placements, including Search, Display, Discover, and YouTube inventory through Performance Max.

The campaign concept targets potential commercial buyers actively looking for bulk sauces, wholesale suppliers, and restaurant condiment solutions.

### 3. Search Intent and Ad Copy

Mapped commercial search themes to advertising messages that communicate:
- Product availability and bulk pack sizes.
- Relevance for restaurants and commercial kitchens.
- Supplier reliability and wholesale purchasing needs.

### 4. Unified Marketing Funnel

**TOFU – Awareness and Discovery**
- Meta awareness campaigns.
- SEO-led blog content.
- KPIs: Reach, impressions, and organic search traffic.

**MOFU – Consideration and Community**
- Instagram and Facebook content.
- Product education and quality-focused storytelling.
- KPIs: Engagement rate, profile visits, and follower growth.

**BOFU – Conversion and Wholesale Enquiries**
- High-intent Google Ads.
- Wholesale enquiry and lead-generation journeys.
- KPIs: Cost per acquisition, enquiry form submissions, and wholesale orders.

## Phase 4: CRM, Automation & Conversational Commerce

### 1. CRM Strategy and Lead Lifecycle

Designed a six-stage customer journey:

1. **Visitor:** Lands on the website.
2. **Lead:** Submits an enquiry form.
3. **Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL):** Shows engagement through email interactions.
4. **Sales Qualified Lead (SQL):** Requests pricing or demonstrates strong buying intent.
5. **Customer:** Places an order.
6. **Repeat Customer:** Purchases again.

The framework connects marketing engagement with sales follow-up and customer retention.

### 2. Customer Segmentation in Mailchimp

Developed a tagging structure across four dimensions:

- **Customer type:** Retailer, restaurant, and home user.
- **Product interest:** Ketchup, chilli sauce, mayonnaise, and soya sauce.
- **Engagement behaviour:** Opened, clicked, and inactive.
- **Lifecycle stage:** Lead, MQL, SQL, and customer.

This structure supports more relevant communication based on customer needs, product preferences, and engagement.

### 3. Automated Welcome Email Series

Designed a three-email welcome journey:

- **Day 0 – Welcome to StarX:** Brand introduction and product overview.
- **Day 2 – Our Bestsellers:** Introduction to ketchup, soya sauce, and chilli sauce.
- **Day 5 – Bulk Orders:** Communication tailored to restaurants and commercial kitchens.

### 4. Lead Nurturing and Behavioural Triggers

Developed a nurture sequence running from Day 7 to Day 30:

- **Day 7:** Product education and range introduction.
- **Day 14:** Special offer and first bulk-order incentive.
- **Day 21:** Restaurant testimonials and social proof.
- **Day 30:** Re-engagement communication and new-launch updates.

Proposed behavioural triggers include:
- Email opens triggering engagement tags and relevant follow-ups.
- Product-link clicks prompting product-specific communication.
- Inactivity triggering re-engagement emails.
- Enquiry form submissions updating lead status and alerting the sales team.

### 5. Conversational Commerce and Chatbot Design

Designed chatbot use cases for the StarX website using Wix and Tidio.

**Key use cases:**
- **Product enquiry:** Display relevant product details and enquiry links.
- **Bulk-order lead capture:** Collect customer name, email, and required quantity.
- **Frequently asked questions:** Address common queries concerning food safety certification, delivery, and minimum order quantities.
- **Callback requests:** Capture contact details and notify the relevant team.

**Human handoff logic**

The proposed workflow transfers complex pricing requests, custom-order enquiries, and frustrated customers to a human representative. Outside business hours, the chatbot captures callback details and supports lead classification.
